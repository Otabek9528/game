# Publishing Muslim Vegukin on Google Play

The step-by-step guide for a first release is `play-launch-guide.html` in this folder (open it in a browser), also
published at <https://claude.ai/artifact/P2kPR1voURpzxPQU5P6bhe>. It covers, in order: the privacy policy page,
the developer account, building the `.aab`, every Play Console form with its answers, the store texts in English,
Russian and Uzbek with copy buttons, internal testing, the 12-tester closed test, applying for production and
releasing.

Files it uses, all in this folder: `privacy.html` (goes into the web app's repository, next to `index.html`),
`play_icon_512.png`, `feature_graphic.png`.
